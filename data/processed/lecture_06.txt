Introduction to Generative AI in the Cloud
Transformative Impact
Generative AI is revolutionizing industries by enabling content
creation, data synthesis, and decision support at
unprecedented scale and quality.
Cloud Provider Role
Major cloud providers are democratizing access to GenAI
through managed services, reducing the technical barriers and
infrastructure costs.
Enterprise Adoption
By 2025, over 80% of enterprises are integrating GenAI into
their workflows, with cloud platforms serving as the primary
deployment vehicle.
